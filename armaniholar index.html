About us

Driven by purpose

ARMANIHOLAR is a non-governmental organisation based in Lagos, Nigeria. We believe every person deserves the opportunity to live with dignity, hope and purpose.

Our approach is simple: listen to communities, support people and create practical opportunities for positive change.
